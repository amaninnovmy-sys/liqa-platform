# LIQA | لِقا

**Field research and participating-office management**  
A venture by AMAN Innovation Group — Malaysia.

## Scope

Two Arabic-first, responsive workspaces are being implemented from the approved LIQA designs:

- **Administrator:** participating offices, assignment to field marketers, interviews, trial participation, follow-ups, subscription oversight, and an audit trail.
- **Field marketer:** assigned offices, consent-based registration, interviews, next actions, and trial invitations.

The proposed monthly subscription is **SAR 196**. This is a price to test, not evidence of willingness to pay. Participation in research, account activation, and a paid subscription are separate events.

## Status

Repository initialized for the first implementation. An operational release is **not yet deployed**. Demo records must be clearly distinguished from actual offices, payments, and research findings.

## Security and research boundaries

- Never commit `.env` files, credentials, personal office/customer records, interview recordings, or payment receipts.
- Do not turn research participation into a subscription without explicit agreement.
- Do not treat the illustrative 70% / 55% / 20% figures as measured research outcomes.
- Trial invitations shown in a demo are not sent to real recipients.
- UI role switching is not production authorization; production requires server-side authentication, authorization, and data isolation.
- Actual collection of subscriptions remains disabled until merchant, accounting, and operating requirements are approved.
